As shown in \Cref{tab:main_results_all}, the OOS F1 scores of all methods decrease as KIR increases, because denser known-intent distributions increase the semantic overlap between in-domain and OOS samples. The proposed method, however, maintains relatively stable OOS F1 scores under different KIR settings. This suggests that localized cluster-wise rejection is more robust to intent-space densification. The improvements are particularly prominent on Banking77, where localized sub-centroids fit fine-grained semantic boundaries more precisely. The trade-off is that strict front-end rejection slightly compromises Known F1, since some marginal in-domain samples are classified as OOS to preserve robust OOS rejection.
